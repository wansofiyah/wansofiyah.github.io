About

I work at the intersection of computer vision, LLMs, and mobile/edge deployment, taking a model from a training notebook to something that runs reliably in a plantation with patchy internet, or a document pipeline that has to work on messy, real-world scans. At SawitPRO, I develop CV and geospatial features on top of drone orthophoto data, build LLM-based document extraction workflows, and prototype Android apps that put those models in the hands of field teams.

Earlier, at PTPN IV, I coordinated data collection for a heavy equipment inventory system across 13 plantation units. I care about shipping models that are honestly evaluated and genuinely useful in the field, not just accurate on paper, and about explaining what a model did and why, whether that's a Grad-CAM heatmap or a plain-language note for a non-technical stakeholder.

Sawit Vision: Palm Tree Detection & Analytics

acc 97.4%
Computer Vision · SawitPRO, Dec 2025 - Present

Engineered a palm tree detection pipeline with tiled inference on high-resolution drone orthophotos, then extended the core detector into 5 downstream analytics features for plantation monitoring.

  • Computer Vision
  • GIS
  • Remote Sensing
  • Machine Learning
  • QGIS
  • UAV
Type & RoleWork project at SawitPRO. I owned the detection pipeline end to end, from tiled inference to the downstream analytics features.
ImpactCut SLA by 90% and mandays cost by 36% for tree-counting work that used to be done manually from drone footage.
What I learnedHow to make a CV model behave on data it wasn't designed for at first: gigapixel drone orthophotos that don't fit in memory as one image. Tiling, overlap, and non-max suppression across tiles turned out to matter more than the detector architecture itself.

FFB Grader: Automated Ripeness Classification

recall 94%
Mobile + Edge AI · Android, Kotlin

Built an offline-first Android app for automated FFB ripeness grading, enabling field-based capture and NFC peer-to-peer data transfer without internet connectivity, including on-device model format trade-offs (TFLite vs. PTL) and quantization.

  • Android Development
  • Kotlin
  • NFC
Type & RoleWork project at SawitPRO. I built the app individually, from the on-device model integration to the NFC transfer flow.
ImpactLets field workers grade ripeness and log data on-site, with zero internet dependency, at 94% recall.
What I learnedDeploying a model is a different problem from training one. I had to think about battery, storage, and the fact that a plantation block often has no signal at all, which pushed me toward on-device inference and offline-first design instead of the cloud-API approach I'd normally default to.

Marketing Campaign Effectiveness: A/B Testing

stat. sig.
Statistical Analysis · Individual Project

Analyzed marketing campaign performance using ANOVA and Bonferroni-corrected t-tests to identify the top-performing strategy for a new product launch.

  • ANOVA
  • A/B Testing
  • Excel
Type & RoleIndividual project, self-directed.
ImpactProduced a clear recommendation (Campaign 1 or 3 over Campaign 2) for a simulated product launch, backed by statistically significant results.
What I learnedRunning multiple comparisons without correction inflates false positives. Adding the Bonferroni correction on top of ANOVA changed which differences I could actually trust, which was a good, concrete lesson in not overclaiming from data.
